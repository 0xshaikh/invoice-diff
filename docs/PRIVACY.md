# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

**invoice-diff** is a local command-line tool. By design, it does not collect, transmit, or store any data.

## What we do

- Your CSV files stay on your machine. `invoice-diff` reads them locally, produces a report locally, and exits.
- We have no servers. There is nothing to log you against.
- We have no analytics, no tracking cookies, no pixel calls.
- Nothing you reconcile is ever seen by 0xshaikh.

## What we do not do

- Sell, share, or broker your data to any third party.
- Use your inputs to train any model.
- Store recovered amounts, order IDs, or GSTINs anywhere.
- Contact you unless you contact us first via GitHub Issues.

## Reporting

If you believe a version of `invoice-diff` exhibits behavior inconsistent with this policy, open a confidential issue at:

**reports@0xshaikh.dev**

Please do not include customer data in the report — describe the behavior, not the input.

## Scope

This policy applies to the open-source project hosted at github.com/0xshaikh/invoice-diff. It does not apply to forks unless the fork maintainer explicitly extends this policy.
